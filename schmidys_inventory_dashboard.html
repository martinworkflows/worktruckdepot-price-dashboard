Schmidy's Equipment Buyer Reference
Under CDL bucket trucks
Altec LR756 (61ft) and LR758 (63ft) on Freightliner M2 / Ford F750 / Hino 338 — all under 26K GVWR, no CDL required. Ideal for municipal and contractor fleets avoiding CDL overhead.
Elevator vs. rear mount
Elevator: boom folds over cab (AN67-E100 = 105ft, LR7-60E70 = 75ft). Rear mount: boom mounts behind cab (LR760-70 = 75ft). Elevators offer greater reach; rear mounts are lower profile.
Altec crane series — AC vs. AM
AC = aerial crane (AC35, AC38, AC40 series) — 35–40+ ton rated, 127–210ft sheave. AM = aerial material handler (AM55) — insulated, lighter duty. Match tonnage to your lift specs.
Digger derrick configurations
DC47-TR / DM47B-TR = 47ft sheave, distribution class. 4x4 chassis preferred for storm/ROW work. Track-mounted (Panther T16, SDP 50BA) for backyard and off-road access without road permits.
Chip truck body sizing
11ft Southco / Altec chip bodies on M2 = Under CDL. Pair with LR-series aerial for full tree-trimming package. Separate chipper units (Altec DC1317) available for stand-alone chipping operations.
Chassis selection guide
Freightliner M2 106 = Class 6–7, CDL. Ford F750 / Hino 338 = Class 6, borderline CDL. Ford F550 / International 4300 = Class 4–5, sub-CDL. Peterbilt 365 / 379 = heavy haul crane platform.
